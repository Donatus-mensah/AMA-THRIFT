Our Story

Ama Thrift and Clothing was born from a passion for sustainable, unique fashion. Founded on the belief that a great outfit expresses individuality, we set out to curate high-quality, pre-loved clothes that tell a story.

Our Mission & Values

To provide unforgettable fashion experiences through exceptional thrift collections, combining affordability with modern style. We are committed to quality, sustainability, and celebrating individuality.

  • ✓ Quality: Using only the finest, carefully inspected pieces.
  • ✓ Sustainability: Promoting a circular fashion economy.
  • ✓ Customer Delight: Ensuring a personal and memorable shopping experience.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are your delivery options?

We offer free delivery within Accra and a standard flat fee for regions outside Accra.

Are the thrift items washed and clean?

Yes, all our items go through a rigorous quality check and are professionally laundered before being listed.

What is your return policy?

Due to the nature of thrift clothing, we generally do not accept returns unless the item has a major undisclosed defect. We encourage checking sizes carefully!

How can I track my order?

Once your order is processed, you can use your unique Order ID on our tracking page to check its real-time status.
